# HUGO KID 2.9.1 game curriculum and legacy audit

The 20 game campaigns contain **474 episodes and 1,482 counted in-game activities**. Safari with Nuru is a separate seven-episode story with **18 field decisions** and **five practical story tasks** in Tembo’s Fruit Salad.

**How to read the map.** Difficulty runs from 1 (foundation) to 6 (application across several clues). Safari uses levels 1–7 in story order. Item counts cover the authored activities wired into the game. Entry practice, shuffled choice orders, coins, obstacles, reusable recipe art and procedurally placed obstacles are excluded. An item can be a question, a matching pair or a writing prompt. The story’s kitchen episode counts five practical actions.

The audit found recurring rounds, small banks or repeated mechanics in all 20 games. The replacement column describes what changed in each game. Several games keep their existing physical controls and practical interactions. Topics can recur across different games to reinforce a skill. The same question prompt and context do not recur within a game’s campaign.

## Legacy content audit

| Game | Content in 2.9.0 | Content in 2.9.1 |
|---|---|---|
| Racing | 2.9.0 supplied a shared maths pool with a colour or arithmetic fallback. | Five episode-specific road gates. Counting progresses to fractions and multi-step planning. Driving, traffic and car rewards remain. |
| Savannah Runner | The endless-style obstacle pattern and speed were the main changes across episodes. | Wildlife field decisions at distance milestones and before rewards. Jumping and obstacle avoidance continue between stops. |
| Penalty Shootout | Goalkeeper skill rose while shooting and saving repeated. | Episode-specific match maths between turns. Taking and saving penalties remain. |
| Little Pilot | Ring count, speed and bird hazards increased without a planned lesson sequence. | Science notebook decisions during flight. Ring navigation and steering remain. |
| Build Your Village | The same building catalogue and stock cost targets were scaled by level. | Planning inspections teach maps, structures, budgets, fair tests and accessibility. The building grid and budget remain. |
| Cooking with Mum | Seven recipes rotated from episode to episode. | Science investigations while serving dishes, plus batch scaling in later episodes. Existing recipes and pictures remain as a practical activity. |
| Colour Bubbles | The same cluster rule repeated with different layouts and scoring targets. | Cluster milestones introduce the episode’s maths. Board generation and popping stay in the game. |
| Nuru’s Jungle Quest | All forks drew from an age-based arithmetic generator. | Four English activities at the forks. Creative episodes include a free-writing station. Running, dodging and retrying forks remain. |
| Wildlife Rescue | Early rescue clues repeated. Later levels largely asked the child to find a named animal. | Field identification, habitats, diets, classification, ecosystems and conservation replace the original rounds. |
| My Little Farm | Seeds, water and harvest counting repeated in a short loop. | Observation, living things, body, energy, environment and experiments replace the repeated rounds. |
| Build a Bridge | Most rounds asked how many planks were missing. | Crossing choices progress from position and sequencing to structural design, evidence and constraints. |
| Ocean Explorer | A small repeating set of compass directions and destinations dominated the route. | Coastal and aquatic wildlife decisions replace the direction loop. |
| Number Balloons | Counting dots and age-based arithmetic dominated all levels. | The episode objective drives the actual balloon answers. The moving-balloon interaction remains. |
| Missing Letters | A small fixed spelling list was shuffled, with different templates by age. | Episodes introduce new English concepts in the quiz itself, ending with draft writing. |
| Safari Memory | Icons, vocabulary pictures and words came from reusable decks. | The three clue-and-answer pairs are built from the episode’s animal topic. Card matching remains. |
| Robot Route | Maps and command capacity varied while the route task stayed similar. | Learning stops along planned and manual routes add mapping, building, debugging, tests and constraints. |
| Little Market | Fruit requests and change practice drew from recurring generators. | Episode-specific arithmetic between customers, with older orders and payment mechanics kept for practical play. |
| Shape Puzzle | Free placement reused forms. The older advanced quiz reused a small set of shape prompts. | Early shape placement includes numeracy checks. Later episodes switch to objective-led maths questions. |
| Say It to Open | Each episode rotated through familiarity-based vocabulary categories. | Door clues are drawn from the episode’s English objective, ending with writing. Android speech and tap answers share the clues. |
| Faith Journey | One religion-specific question deck was reshuffled throughout the campaign. | Values cases progress through care, fairness, honesty, cooperation, stewardship and leadership. The existing faith-profile lesson remains available. |

**Safari with Nuru.** The earlier story mixed animal clues, shape recognition, letters and simple counting over five follow-up chapters. The new six follow-up missions make a single wildlife sequence. The cooking introduction and previously earned passport stamps are retained.
